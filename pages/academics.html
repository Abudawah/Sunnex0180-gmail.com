Beyond the Classroom

Co-Curricular Activities

We believe education extends far beyond the classroom. Our co-curricular programme develops leadership, creativity, teamwork, and physical fitness.

01

Sports & Athletics

Football, athletics, basketball, volleyball, table tennis, and annual inter-house sports competitions. Physical fitness and teamwork are central to our school culture.

02

Debate & Public Speaking

Our debate and oratory club trains students to articulate ideas, argue persuasively, and speak with confidence. Regular inter-school competitions keep standards high.

03

Cultural & Drama

Yoruba cultural club, drama troupe, dance, and music — students express creativity and preserve cultural heritage through performance and the arts.

04

Science & Tech Club

Robotics, coding basics, science experiments, and state science competitions. We cultivate curiosity, problem-solving, and STEM passion.

05

Student Government

Elected student council, prefect body, and house captain system develop leadership, governance, and responsibility in senior students.

06

Community Service

Regular community outreach, environmental care programmes, and charity drives teach empathy, citizenship, and the importance of giving back.
