Our History

Born from a Bold Vision

On May 5, 2010, Al-Lateef Group of Schools opened its gates in Ifo, Ogun State with 80 pioneering students and a team of 12 dedicated staff. Founded with one powerful goal: to give every child in Ifo access to a world-class education built on discipline, strong values, and academic rigour.

The name "Al-Lateef" — meaning "The Gentle" and "The Kind" — reflects the care and depth with which we approach every child's development. From a single building with three classrooms, we have grown into a full educational group serving over 1,200 students.

Our 5,000+ alumni are found in universities, professions, and leadership roles across Nigeria and beyond — proof of what 15 years of purposeful education produces.

Proprietor's Welcome Address

A Word of Welcome

Alhaji S.A. Al-Lateef · Founder & Proprietor
"Education is the greatest investment a family can make. At Al-Lateef, we have dedicated fifteen years to ensuring that investment yields exceptional returns — not just in grades, but in character, confidence, and purpose."

Welcome to Al-Lateef Group of Schools. When I founded this institution on May 5, 2010, I carried a simple but powerful belief: that every child in Ifo, and in Nigeria, deserves an education that truly prepares them for life — not just for examinations.

Over the past fifteen years, I have watched hundreds of children walk through our gates as curious young learners and leave as confident, principled, and capable young adults. I have seen our alumni go on to study Medicine, Law, Engineering, and Business at top Nigerian universities and abroad.

As we mark our 15th anniversary, I invite every parent, student, and member of our community to renew their commitment to the values and vision that have carried us this far. The best of Al-Lateef is still ahead of us. Welcome to our family.

Alhaji S.A. Al-Lateef
Founder & Proprietor, Al-Lateef Group of Schools · Est. May 5, 2010

15 Years of Growth

Our Milestones

From 80 students in 3 classrooms to 1,200 students across two schools — a journey of purpose, perseverance, and pride.

May 5, 2010

School Founded

Al-Lateef Nursery & Primary opens in Ifo with 80 pioneering students, 12 staff members, and a mission to transform education in the community.

2012

First Primary Graduation

Our first Primary 6 graduates proceed to secondary schools across Ogun State. The community celebrates with tremendous pride.

2013

Model College Established

Al-Lateef Model College opens, providing a natural progression for our primary graduates. JSS 1 commences with 45 students.

2015

New Campus Facilities

New classroom blocks, staff room, and a multipurpose hall commissioned. Enrolment crosses 400 students for the first time.

2016

First WAEC — 100% Pass Rate

Al-Lateef Model College records a landmark 100% pass rate in its first WAEC examinations. Three students earn distinctions in all nine subjects.

2018

ICT Lab & Library Opened

A 30-station computer laboratory and a 3,000-book library are opened, greatly expanding learning resources for all students.

2020

10th Anniversary & Science Block

The school celebrates 10 years with a new science lab block, home economics room, and arts studio. Enrolment exceeds 900 students.

2023

E-Learning Platform Launched

Al-Lateef launches its digital learning platform — students can now access lessons, assignments, and resources from anywhere, anytime.

May 5, 2025

15 Years of Excellence

Al-Lateef marks 15 years with 1,200+ students, 5,000+ alumni, 98% WAEC pass rate, and a reputation as one of the finest schools in Ogun State. The best is still ahead.
