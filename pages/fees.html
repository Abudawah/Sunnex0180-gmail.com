Payment FAQs

Common Payment Questions

Can I pay school fees in instalments?

+
Full term fees are due before the first day of each term. However, in genuine hardship cases, parents may speak with the Bursar's office to arrange a structured payment plan. Please contact the school before the term begins.

How long does it take for payment to be confirmed?

+
Online payments via card, bank transfer, and USSD are confirmed instantly. You will receive a receipt by email within 1–2 minutes. If you do not receive a receipt after 30 minutes, please contact the school with your transaction reference number.

Can I pay at the school's physical office?

+
Yes. You may pay in cash or by bank transfer directly at the Bursar's office, Monday to Friday between 8am and 3pm. Please request an official receipt at the time of payment.

Is it safe to pay with my bank card on this website?

+
Absolutely. All card payments are processed by Paystack — one of Nigeria's most trusted and PCI-DSS compliant payment processors. We never see or store your card details. The payment page is protected by 256-bit SSL encryption.

What happens if I pay the wrong amount?

+
If you pay an incorrect amount, please contact the school Bursar immediately with your transaction reference. Overpayments are credited to the following term. Underpayments must be topped up before your child is admitted to class for the new term.
