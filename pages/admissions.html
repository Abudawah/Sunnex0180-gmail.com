Required Documents

  • Completed application form (fully signed)
  • Recent passport photograph (2 copies)
  • Birth certificate or age declaration
  • Last school report card (2 copies)
  • School leaving certificate (if applicable)
  • Parent/Guardian valid ID
  • Immunisation card (for Nursery intake)
  • Application fee receipt

Application Fees

  • Nursery & Kindergarten — ₦2,000
  • Primary 1–6 — ₦3,000

  • SSS 1–3 — ₦5,000

Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers

What age is required to start Nursery 1?

+
Children must be at least 2 years 6 months old at the start of the academic session to enrol in Nursery 1. We also accept children who turn 3 years within the first term of the session.

When does the academic session begin and end?

+
The 2025/2026 session begins on July 15, 2025 and ends in June 2026. The school year is divided into three terms, each approximately 13 weeks long. Exact term dates are published in the school calendar.

Is there an entrance examination for all classes?

+
Yes. All applicants from Kindergarten upwards sit an entrance examination covering English Language, Mathematics, and General Knowledge. The exam is designed to be fair and age-appropriate. Nursery admissions are based on age requirements and an informal assessment.

What are the school fees for 2025/2026?

+
Nursery & Primary tuition starts from ₦45,000 per term, and Model College from ₦65,000 per term. Fees cover tuition and use of facilities. A detailed fee breakdown is available on the Pay Fees page or in the prospectus. Additional charges may apply for uniforms, textbooks, and extra-curricular activities.

Do you offer scholarships or bursaries?

+
Yes. Al-Lateef awards merit-based scholarships to outstanding students in JSS 1 and SSS 1 entry classes. We also have a bursary fund for students facing genuine financial hardship. Please contact our admissions office for details on eligibility and the application procedure.

Is transport provided to and from school?

+
Yes. We partner with a reputable school bus service covering several routes in Ifo and surrounding areas. Please enquire at the admissions office for current routes, schedules, and transport fees.

Can I transfer my child mid-session?

+
Mid-session transfers are considered on a case-by-case basis, subject to space availability in the relevant class. Please contact our admissions office with your child's most recent school report and other required documents to begin the assessment process.
