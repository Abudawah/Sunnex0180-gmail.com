Portal FAQs

I forgot my portal password. What do I do?

+
Click "Forgot Password" on the login screen and enter your registered email address. A reset link will be sent within 5 minutes. If you don't receive it, check your spam folder or contact the admin office.

How do I get my Student ID?

+
Your Student ID is printed on your school ID card and at the top of every report card (format: AL-YYYY-XXX). If you can't find it, the admin office can provide it immediately.

Can I access the portal on my phone?

+
Yes. All portals are fully mobile-responsive and work on any modern smartphone or tablet browser. No app download is required — simply open your browser and log in.

I'm a new parent — how do I register?

+
After your child is enrolled, the admin office will send you a welcome email with your initial login credentials. You can then set your own password using the "Forgot Password" link on the login page.
