Our Story

15 Years of Shaping Futures

Founded on May 5, 2010 in Ifo, Ogun State, Al-Lateef started with 80 students and a bold vision — quality education built on discipline and strong values. Today, 15 years on, over 1,200 students study across two thriving schools.

2010

School founded — Nursery & Primary opens May 5 with 80 students.

2013

Model College launched — Secondary division opens for growing alumni.

2016

First WAEC — 100% pass rate — A landmark achievement.

2020

10-Year milestone — New science lab, library & ICT centre.

2025

15 Years! — 5,000+ alumni, 1,200 students. A legacy to be proud of.
